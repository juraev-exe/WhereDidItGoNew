Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 Developer: WhereDidItGo (Juraev.exe) Contact: support@wherediditgo.app

1. Immediate Self-Service Deletion (In-App)

Because WhereDidItGo operates on a 100% local-first architecture, you hold direct, unilateral control over all stored data without needing to submit a ticket or wait for administrator approval:

  1. Launch the WhereDidItGo app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings gear tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Reset all local data.
  5. Confirm the prompt. All stored transactions, balances, categories, debts, and savings goals will be permanently and irreversibly purged from your device's IndexedDB database.

2. Deletion by Application Uninstallation

Uninstalling the WhereDidItGo application through Android, iOS, or your desktop operating system immediately wipes the sandboxed local storage directory, permanently deleting all locally held data.

3. Requesting Erasure of Email Correspondence

We do not operate backend user accounts or cloud databases storing your financial numbers. However, if you have corresponded with our developer support desk via email or sent feedback:

  • You can request full erasure of all email correspondence and support logs.
  • Send an email to support@wherediditgo.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  • Our team will expunge and permanently shred all email message threads associated with your address within 30 calendar days.

4. In-App Purchase Records

Purchase tokens and transaction receipts processed via Google Play or the Apple App Store are managed by Google LLC and Apple Inc. pursuant to tax regulations and commercial record retention laws. Consult your Google Play account or Apple ID billing settings to review or delete payment history.
