Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 Developer: WhereDidItGo (Juraev.exe) Contact: support@wherediditgo.app

1. Local-First Storage & Optional Cloud Vault Sync

WhereDidItGo ("we", "our", or "us") operates on a local-first architectural model. Your financial records stay on your device by default:

  • On-Device Records: All transaction records, income logs, expenses, category budgets, debts, savings goals, and transaction notes are stored in your device's isolated IndexedDB local storage sandbox.
  • No Account Required by Default: You do not need to register, create a username, or provide an email address to use the application offline.
  • Optional Cloud Vault Sync (Opt-In): If you explicitly choose to create an account and enable Cloud Vault sync, your authentication session and backed-up ledger records (accounts, categories, budgets, debts, recurring items, and transactions) are encrypted in transit and stored in a Row-Level Security (RLS) isolated Supabase database tied strictly to your account ID.

2. Permissions & On-Device Processing (Data Minimization)

We request only the operating system capabilities required for the features you invoke:

  • No Location or Contacts Access: We never track, request, or record GPS locations, contacts, or microphone audio.
  • On-Device Receipt Scanning (OCR): When you use the optional Receipt Scanner, camera frames or chosen photos stay on your device and are processed locally by Tesseract.js. Receipt images are never uploaded to our servers. On first use, static OCR language packs (eng / rus) may be downloaded from jsDelivr / Project Naptha CDN without sending personal data.
  • Permitted OS Features:
    • CAMERA — accessed only when you choose to scan a paper receipt.
    • VIBRATE — provides subtle haptic clicks during keypad and action interactions.
    • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — allows optional local budget and recurring bill reminders.
    • INTERNET — used for Pro store/web billing verification, optional user-initiated Cloud Vault sync, free-tier mobile ads, and first-time OCR language pack download.
    • AD_ID (Android) — Google Play advertising identifier used solely on free-tier native mobile builds when displaying ads.

3. In-App Purchases, Web Billing & No Hidden Fees

WhereDidItGo offers an optional upgrade ("WhereDidItGo Pro") on Lifetime, Annual, and Monthly plans:

  • Purchases are processed directly through the Google Play Store (Android), Apple App Store (iOS), or RevenueCat Web Billing / Stripe (Web).
  • We never see, collect, or store your full payment card numbers, CVV codes, or banking credentials.
  • We use RevenueCat solely to validate purchase receipts and Pro entitlement status. Offline users receive an anonymous RevenueCat ID; users who sign into Cloud Vault sync have their Supabase account ID associated in RevenueCat so Pro unlocks follow across signed-in devices.
  • Transparent Pricing: Plan pricing and billing intervals are shown clearly before confirmation. Auto-renewing Annual or Monthly plans can be canceled anytime through Google Play, Apple ID, or your Web billing receipt link.

4. Third-Party SDKs & Free-Tier Ads

We audit our dependencies regularly to keep data collection minimal:

  • Free-Tier Native Mobile Ads: Free native mobile builds integrate Google AdMob (@capacitor-community/admob). Purchasing WhereDidItGo Pro disables all ad requests permanently. Web and Desktop builds include zero ad network SDKs.
  • No Behavioral Profiling or Data Brokers: We do not embed Firebase Analytics, Meta Pixel, AppsFlyer, or sell user financial records to data brokers.
  • Review our complete Third-Party SDK Audit for itemized technical breakdowns.

5. Age Consent & Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

WhereDidItGo is designed for general audiences aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because the application operates without mandatory accounts or behavioral profiling, no profiles of children are intentionally compiled.

6. Email Communications & Anti-Spam (Unsubscribe Guarantee)

We respect your inbox:

  • We do not send unsolicited promotional marketing emails or sell your email address.
  • When you email our support desk at support@wherediditgo.app, your email is used solely to answer your question.
  • If any optional email announcement list is made available in the future, every message will feature a prominent, one-click Unsubscribe link complying with the CAN-SPAM Act and GDPR Article 7.

7. Data Deletion & Your Legal Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have full autonomy over your data under GDPR (Articles 15–22) and CCPA:

  • Instant Self-Service Local Erasure: Inside the app, go to Settings → Danger Zone → Reset all local data to permanently wipe all on-device IndexedDB records immediately.
  • Optional Cloud Vault Erasure: If you signed into Cloud Vault sync, consult our Data Deletion Guide to request permanent removal of your Supabase authentication account and synced cloud backup rows.
  • Uninstalling the App: Deleting the app from your device automatically destroys the application's isolated local database.
  • Data Export: You can export full JSON backups or CSV files at any time.
  • Correspondence Erasure: To request deletion of any support email correspondence, consult our Data Deletion Guide or email support@wherediditgo.app.

8. Cookies & Web Local Storage

Our website does not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We store strictly necessary local preferences — theme (wdg-theme), language (wdg-locale), cookie notice state (wdg-cookie-consent) — plus your Supabase authentication token only if you explicitly sign in to Cloud Vault sync. For more information, see our Cookie Policy.
