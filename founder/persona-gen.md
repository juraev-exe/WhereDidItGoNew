# Customer personas: WhereDidItGo

## Persona 1: Alex the Privacy Advocate
- Profile: Software engineer or remote digital worker, age 28-40.
- Pain point: Dislikes linking primary bank accounts to cloud services after recurring data breach headlines. Frustrated by budgeting apps that require email signups before viewing the home screen.
- Willingness to pay: High. Gladly pays $26.97 for a permanent lifetime license to support independent, private software.
- Key hook: "No account required. All data stays in your browser and on your phone."

## Persona 2: Elena the Cross-Border Freelancer
- Profile: International consultant or freelancer earning in USD and EUR while living in Eastern Europe or Central Asia, age 24-35.
- Pain point: Traditional local banking apps fail at tracking mixed currencies and cash transactions. Needs quick receipt capture for tax deductibles.
- Willingness to pay: Moderate. Chooses the $9.29 annual subscription for receipt scanning and cloud sync across phone and laptop.
- Key hook: "Multi-currency tracking, instant receipt scanning, zero cloud lock-in."

## Persona 3: Farrukh the Family Budgeter
- Profile: Small business operator or household financial manager in Central Asia (Tajikistan / Uzbekistan / Kazakhstan), age 30-50.
- Pain point: Western financial apps lack local language support (Tajik) and enforce expensive subscription fees ($10/mo). Frequently lends money to relatives and needs to track informal debts.
- Willingness to pay: Price sensitive. Uses the free ad-supported tier or watches rewarded video ads for occasional Pro unlocks.
- Key hook: "Full Tajik and Russian translation, informal debt tracking, works 100% offline."\n
