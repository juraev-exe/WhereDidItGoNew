Refund Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 Developer: WhereDidItGo (Juraev.exe) Support Desk: support@wherediditgo.app

1. Pricing Transparency & Plan Options

WhereDidItGo Pro is offered as a one-time Lifetime unlock or optional auto-renewing Annual / Monthly plans. The exact price and billing interval are shown before confirmation, and subscriptions can be canceled before renewal with zero hidden fees.

2. Google Play Store Refunds (Android)

If you purchased WhereDidItGo Pro through Google Play on Android:

  • Within 48 hours of purchase: You can submit an automated refund request directly through Google Play by visiting your Google Play Order History. Select WhereDidItGo Pro → Request a refund. Google usually processes decisions within 1 business day.
  • After 48 hours: If you run into technical issues or purchase discrepancies after the 48-hour window, contact our developer support team at support@wherediditgo.app with your Google Play Order Number (formatted as GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX). We assess developer refund exceptions fairly and promptly.

3. Apple App Store Refunds (iOS)

If you purchased WhereDidItGo Pro on an iPhone or iPad:

  • Apple manages all payment transactions, billing, and refunds directly. Developers do not have access to Apple customer billing records or unilateral refund triggers.
  • To request a refund from Apple, navigate to reportaproblem.apple.com, log in with your Apple ID, select "Request a refund", and choose WhereDidItGo.

4. Web Billing Refunds (Stripe / RevenueCat Web)

If you purchased WhereDidItGo Pro through our Web app checkout (processed by Stripe / RevenueCat Web Billing):

  • 14-Day Refund Request: Email support@wherediditgo.app within 14 days of purchase with your checkout email address and receipt/invoice number, and we will issue a refund promptly via Stripe.
  • Cancel Renewal: You can cancel Annual or Monthly web renewals at any time from your Web billing customer portal link or by contacting support prior to the next billing date.

5. European Union & UK Statutory Rights

Residents of the European Union and the United Kingdom enjoy statutory consumer withdrawal rights in accordance with EU Consumer Rights Directive regulations, subject to digital goods activation terms.

6. Customer Support & Assistance

Encountering an issue with your Pro unlock or have a billing question? We are here to help:

  • Support Email: support@wherediditgo.app
  • Response Window: 1 to 2 business days.
