Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 Developer: WhereDidItGo (Juraev.exe) Contact: support@wherediditgo.app

1. No Third-Party Tracking or Advertising Cookies

WhereDidItGo believes that web tracking is unnecessary and invasive. We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or cross-site analytics scripts. We do not monetize your browsing habits or share visitor data with data brokers.

2. What We Store: Strictly Essential Local Storage

Instead of tracking cookies, our website utilizes standard browser localStorage purely to remember your chosen interface settings:

Storage Key Category Function / Purpose Duration
wdg-theme Strictly Necessary Remembers your selected appearance (Light Mode vs Dark Mode). Persistent until cleared
wdg-locale Strictly Necessary Remembers your preferred language (English, Russian, or Tajik). Persistent until cleared
wdg-cookie-consent Compliance / Essential Remembers that you have dismissed the cookie and privacy disclosure notice. Persistent until cleared

3. Managing and Deleting Your Storage

You are always in total control of what is stored on your device:

  • Browser Settings: You can wipe all local storage keys anytime through your browser's "Clear Browsing Data" menu.
  • Incognito / Private Mode: Browsing our website in Private mode ensures all storage is automatically cleared when you close the tab.

4. Inquiries & Feedback

Questions regarding our cookie practices can be directed to our support team at support@wherediditgo.app.
