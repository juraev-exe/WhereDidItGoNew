# Go-to-market plan: WhereDidItGo

## 1. Pre-launch phase (Days 1 to 30)
- Google Play Store asset readiness: Complete 16:9 vertical screenshots, 512x512 app icon, and 1024x500 feature graphic.
- ASO optimization: Target high-intent, low-difficulty search queries: "offline budget tracker", "private expense tracker", "expense tracker no login", "учет расходов офлайн".
- Localization QA: Verify Tajik, Russian, and English strings across Android devices.

## 2. Launch execution (Days 31 to 45)
- Product Hunt launch: Emphasize local-first architecture and developer privacy angle.
- Reddit community outreach: Share authentic development journey on r/selfhosted, r/androidapps, r/personalfinance, and r/vuejs.
- Regional Telegram distribution: Announce in Central Asian tech and entrepreneur Telegram channels where Tajik and Russian localization provides immediate novelty.

## 3. Growth and activation loops (Days 46 to 90)
- In-app sharing loop: Add "Share monthly summary graphic" allowing users to export a private visual card of their savings progress to Instagram or WhatsApp.
- Review prompt timing: Trigger Google In-App Review API only after a user successfully logs their 15th transaction or completes their first weekly budget cycle.
- Content distribution: Publish short comparison articles: "Why we built a budget app that does not connect to your bank".\n
