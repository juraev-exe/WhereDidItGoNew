## 1. Instant Self-Service Data Deletion (Inside the App)

Because WhereDidItGo is **100% local-first**, you do not need to wait for a support ticket or manual approval to wipe your records. You have instant, unilateral power to permanently erase everything directly on your device:

1. Open the **WhereDidItGo** app.
2. Tap the **Settings** tab.
3. Scroll down to the **Danger Zone** section.
4. Tap **Reset all local data**.
5. Read the confirmation warning and confirm the reset action.
6. **Result**: All accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, debts, savings goals, and cached settings are instantaneously and irreversibly deleted from your device's IndexedDB database.

---

## 2. Complete Uninstallation

Uninstalling WhereDidItGo from your Android device, iPhone, or computer immediately and permanently destroys the app's isolated local sandbox. The operating system erases all local database tables and preferences.

---

## 3. Requesting Deletion of Support Correspondence & Billing Data

We do not operate backend servers holding your financial transactions. However, if you have interacted with our team via email support or submitted a bug report:
* You may request permanent deletion of your support email records and correspondence history.
* **How to request**: Send an email to `support@wherediditgo.app` with the subject line `Data Deletion Request`.
* **Processing Time**: All correspondence deletion requests are confirmed and permanently expunged within **30 calendar days**.
* **Financial Transactions Note**: For in-app purchases processed through Google Play or Apple App Store, transaction receipts and billing records are maintained by Google LLC or Apple Inc. in accordance with tax and legal requirements. Consult Google or Apple account settings to manage your store transaction records.
