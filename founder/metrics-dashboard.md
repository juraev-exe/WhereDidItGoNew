# Metrics dashboard: WhereDidItGo

## 1. North Star metric
**Weekly Active Expense Loggers (WAEL)**: The number of unique users who record at least 3 transactions in a rolling 7-day period.

## 2. Acquisition and onboarding KPIs
- Google Play Store listing conversion rate: Target > 28%.
- First-minute activation rate: Percentage of new installs who log an expense within 60 seconds (Target > 65%).

## 3. Retention and engagement KPIs
- Day 1 retention: Target > 40%.
- Day 7 retention: Target > 22%.
- Day 30 retention: Target > 12%.
- Average transactions logged per active user per week: Target > 8.

## 4. Monetization KPIs
- Free-to-Pro conversion rate: Target 2.5% to 4.0%.
- Rewarded ad pass utilization: Percentage of active free users who watch a rewarded ad weekly (Target > 15%).
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU): Target $0.18 / monthly active user across blended ads and purchases.\n
