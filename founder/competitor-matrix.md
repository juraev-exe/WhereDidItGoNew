# Competitor matrix: WhereDidItGo

## 1. Overview
The personal finance landscape is split between expensive cloud-connected budgeting platforms and legacy offline expense recorders. WhereDidItGo occupies the middle: a fast, local-first app with modern design, client-side receipt OCR, and optional private cloud backups.

## 3. Positioning gaps and whitespace
1. True local privacy with receipt OCR: None of the prominent local-first competitors (Monefy, Cashew) offer on-device optical character recognition for paper receipts.
2. Multi-language Central Asian coverage: Mainstream western trackers neglect Tajik and regional language nuances. WhereDidItGo ships native English, Russian, and Tajik localizations.
3. Flexible monetization: Competing apps force either pure subscription ($99/year YNAB) or aggressive banner placements. WhereDidItGo offers micro-subscriptions ($1.09/mo), accessible lifetime unlocks ($26.97), and 24-hour rewarded ad passes.\n
