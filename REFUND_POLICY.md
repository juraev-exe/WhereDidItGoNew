## 3. Apple App Store Purchases (iOS)

If you purchased WhereDidItGo Pro on an iOS device:
* Under Apple policy, developers cannot directly initiate refunds on iOS accounts. All refunds are handled exclusively by Apple.
* To request a refund from Apple:
  1. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID used for the purchase.
  3. Select **"I'd like to..."** → **"Request a refund"**.
  4. Select the reason and choose WhereDidItGo.
  5. Apple processes and communicates refund determinations directly.
