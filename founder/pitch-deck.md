# Pitch deck structure: WhereDidItGo

## Slide 1: Title
- WhereDidItGo: The local-first personal finance tracker.
- Subtitle: Private, instant, and offline expense management for the modern world.
- Presenter: Juraev.exe

## Slide 2: The problem
- Personal finance software is broken by cloud bloat.
- Users are forced to surrender bank credentials, endure sluggish load times, and pay $100+ annual subscriptions for basic arithmetic.

## Slide 3: The solution
- WhereDidItGo delivers zero-friction financial control:
  1. Instant offline logging on local IndexedDB storage.
  2. On-device receipt scanning without cloud image uploads.
  3. Optional encrypted Cloud Vault sync when users want multi-device access.

## Slide 4: Market opportunity
- $1.5B+ global personal finance market expanding at 11% CAGR.
- Growing consumer distrust of financial data aggregators creates a distinct wedge for privacy-first financial utilities.

## Slide 5: Product and technology
- Vue 3, Vite, TypeScript, and Dexie.js core.
- Capacitor 8 native Android and iOS mobile wrappers with haptics.
- Supabase Row Level Security for optional encrypted cloud backups.
- Production app currently at version 1.1.5.

## Slide 6: Traction and progress
- Production ready codebase deployed across Android and Web.
- Trilingual support implemented (English, Russian, Tajik).
- RevenueCat Play Billing and AdMob monetization pipelines integrated.

## Slide 7: Business model
- Accessible pricing with triple monetization:
  1. Ad-supported free tier with 24-hour rewarded video passes.
  2. Pro subscriptions: $1.09/month or $9.29/year.
  3. Pro lifetime license: $26.97.

## Slide 8: Competitive advantage
- WhereDidItGo vs Competitors:
  - YNAB: Too expensive ($99/yr) and requires cloud.
  - Monefy: Outdated interface without receipt scanning.
  - WhereDidItGo: Modern liquid glassmorphism, instant offline speed, local receipt OCR, and multi-language support.

## Slide 9: Go-to-market strategy
- ASO targeting privacy and offline keywords.
- Developer and privacy community launches (Product Hunt, Reddit, Hacker News).
- Regional distribution in Central Asia leveraging first-mover Tajik localization.

## Slide 10: Financial projections
- Unit economics: > 80% gross margin due to zero mandatory server storage for free users.
- Target milestone: 25,000 active users yielding 1,500 Pro subscribers ($14,000 ARR) plus ad revenue.

## Slide 11: Team
- Juraev.exe: Solo founder and full-stack software engineer.

## Slide 12: The ask and next steps
- Launching on Google Play Store.
- Seeking strategic distribution partners, beta community testers, and early feedback.\n
