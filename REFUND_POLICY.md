WhereDidItGo Pro is available as a one-time Lifetime unlock or as an auto-renewing Annual or Monthly subscription. Every plan displays its exact price and billing cadence before checkout, and recurring subscriptions can be canceled anytime before the next renewal date without penalty.

## Google Play Store Purchases (Android)

If you purchased WhereDidItGo Pro through Google Play on Android, you can request an automated refund within 48 hours of the transaction by opening [Google Play Order History](https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory), locating WhereDidItGo Pro, and selecting **Request a refund**. Google typically processes automated requests within one business day.

If more than 48 hours have elapsed and you encountered a technical defect or billing error, email `support@wherediditgo.app` with your Google Play Order ID (formatted `GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX`) and a short description of the issue so we can review and issue a developer refund through Google Play Console.

## Apple App Store (iOS) and Web Checkout (Stripe)

For purchases made on an iPhone or iPad, Apple processes all billing and refund requests directly; developers cannot trigger refunds inside Apple's billing system. To request an iOS refund, sign in at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/), choose **Request a refund**, select WhereDidItGo, and submit your reason to Apple.

For purchases completed through our Web checkout (processed by RevenueCat Web Billing and Stripe), email `support@wherediditgo.app` within 14 days of purchase with the email address used at checkout and your invoice or receipt number, and we will issue a refund through Stripe. You can also cancel upcoming Annual or Monthly web renewals at any time from your receipt management link or by emailing `support@wherediditgo.app` (responses within 1–2 business days). Consumers in the European Union and United Kingdom retain their statutory withdrawal rights under applicable digital consumer regulations.
