# Idea validation: WhereDidItGo

## 1. The 30-second assessment
Personal finance is a crowded category with high churn, but privacy-focused local-first architecture provides a defensible wedge. Users who abandon heavy cloud trackers regularly seek fast, private tools that work reliably offline.

## 2. The five fatal questions

### Q1: Who is the customer, and would they pay for this today?
The primary customer is a mobile-first professional or self-employed individual who tracks daily expenses but refuses to link bank accounts to third-party cloud aggregators. They will pay a one-time lifetime fee of $26.97 or an annual fee of $9.29 to remove ads and enable receipt scanning and cloud backups.

### Q2: Why has not someone built this already?
Competitors have built expense trackers, but most monetization models rely on harvesting financial data or selling bank-linking subscriptions at $10 to $15 per month. Local-first apps like Cashew exist as open-source projects, but lack built-in receipt OCR, multi-platform sync, or commercial distribution in non-English markets.

### Q3: What is the distribution advantage?
The product features full trilingual localization (English, Russian, Tajik), opening under-served markets in Central Asia and Eastern Europe alongside core western app store traffic. Initial distribution relies on App Store Optimization (ASO) for privacy and offline budgeting terms, paired with personal finance community launches.

### Q4: Can this be a big business, or is it a feature?
At its core, expense tracking is a consumer utility. As a standalone solo venture, 10,000 paid subscribers at $9.29 per year yields $92,900 annual recurring revenue with minimal server overhead. Expansion into small team expense logging or freelance tax preparation provides the path beyond consumer utility.

### Q5: Can the founder actually build this?
Yes. The working production codebase (v1.1.5) already contains the full Vue 3 frontend, Capacitor mobile shell, IndexedDB engine, RevenueCat billing integration, and Supabase synchronization layer.
