Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 Developer: WhereDidItGo (Juraev.exe) Contact: support@wherediditgo.app

1. Immediate Self-Service Local Deletion (In-App)

Because WhereDidItGo operates on a local-first architecture, you hold direct control over all stored on-device data without needing administrator approval:

  1. Launch the WhereDidItGo app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Reset all local data.
  5. Confirm the prompt. All stored transactions, balances, categories, debts, and savings goals will be permanently purged from your device's IndexedDB database.

2. Deletion by Application Uninstallation

Uninstalling the WhereDidItGo application through Android, iOS, or your desktop operating system immediately wipes the sandboxed local storage directory, permanently deleting all locally held data.

3. Deleting Optional Cloud Vault Data & Account (If Enabled)

Cloud Vault sync is strictly optional. If you signed into an account for Supabase Cloud Vault backups and want all synced cloud records and your authentication account erased:

  • Sign out in Settings → Cloud Vault on your devices, and wipe local records via Reset all local data if desired.
  • Send an email from your registered address to support@wherediditgo.app with the subject line "Cloud Vault Account Deletion".
  • We will permanently delete your Supabase user account and all associated synced cloud database rows within 30 calendar days (typically within 48 hours).

4. Requesting Erasure of Email Correspondence

If you have corresponded with our developer support desk via email or sent feedback:

  • You can request full erasure of all email correspondence and support logs.
  • Send an email to support@wherediditgo.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  • Our team will expunge all email message threads associated with your address within 30 calendar days.

5. Purchase & Billing Records

Purchase tokens and transaction receipts processed via Google Play, the Apple App Store, or Stripe / RevenueCat Web Billing are retained by Google LLC, Apple Inc., and Stripe pursuant to tax regulations and commercial record retention laws. Consult your Google Play account, Apple ID, or Stripe billing receipt to review your billing history.
