# TREATMENT — "Every Dollar, On Your Phone Alone"

## Creative Direction
A tactile, rhythm-driven 30-second screen demonstration and motion-graphics piece built around **semantic timing** (Hypit Script Selections and Moments). Every visual cut responds to a spoken financial pain point or a concrete product proof point.

## Timeline & Semantic Structure (30.0s Canvas, 1920×1080 / 9:16 Adaptive)

| Segment | Window | Script Passage & Semantic Anchors | Visual Track & MG Component |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Hook (`seg-hook`)** | `0.0s – 6.0s` | "Most finance apps ask for your bank password `[moment:vault-lock]` before you log a single coffee." | Dark `#000000` canvas. At `moment:vault-lock`, a 4-digit biometric PIN lock shield snaps shut with `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)` deceleration. |
